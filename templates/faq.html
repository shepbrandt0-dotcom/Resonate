{{ faq_intro }}

Who do you work with? +

Candidates, elected officials, and campaign or advocacy teams who need their message to land with voters under thirty. Work is available across the political spectrum.

How is this different from a regular speechwriter? +

Most political writing for younger voters is done by people a generation removed from that audience. Resonate combines competitive speech training with lived fluency in how 18 to 30 year olds actually communicate online and in person.

What is included in a discovery call? +

A short conversation to map the audience, the goal, constraints, and whether the project is a good fit. There is no pressure and no charge for the call.

How do I access my documents? +

After you submit an intake, you receive a portal access code. Log in under Client portal to view status, download deliverables, and leave feedback.

How fast is turnaround? +

Standard projects are typically returned within several business days. Retainer clients and rapid response requests get priority. Exact timing is confirmed on the discovery call.

Do you work with both parties? +

Yes. The focus is on clear communication with younger voters, not on advancing a single party line.

Can I get ongoing help during a campaign? +

Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions cover priority support, multiple deliverables, and a consistent voice across events. See the Pricing page for details.

How do payments work? +

Payment terms are confirmed when the scope is agreed. Discovery calls are free. Project work is typically billed per deliverable or on a subscription basis.
