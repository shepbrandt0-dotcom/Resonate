Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Resonate website and the forms, client portal, and admin tools on it, operated by {{ contact_name }}{% if legal_business_name and "[Add" not in legal_business_name %} ({{ legal_business_name }}){% endif %}.

Information we collect

  • Discovery call requests: name, email, organization, preferred time, and any notes you add.
  • Client intake: name, email, organization, role, audience, goal, existing drafts, key messages, constraints, and tone notes you submit.
  • Client portal: your login is tied to an access code; feedback you leave on delivered work is stored against your account.
  • Payments: when a payment is confirmed (by Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, or card), we log the client name, email, amount, service, and payment method for our own records. We do not collect or store card numbers on this site — card and Apple Pay payments are handled by a separate payment processor.
  • Login sessions: a single essential cookie keeps you signed into the client portal or admin area. See the Cookie Policy for detail.

We only ask for what's needed to deliver the service you requested — we don't collect data beyond what's listed above.

How we use it

To respond to your discovery call or intake request, produce and deliver your speeches, coaching notes, or strategy memos, track project status, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your project content to train any AI system — see our no-AI policy.

Third-party services this site uses

This site loads a few resources directly from third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • Tailwind CSS (styling, loaded from its CDN)
  • Unsplash (one decorative background photo)
  • A QR-code library, loaded only on the phone-demo widget in the admin dashboard and demo page

We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information as long as needed to deliver your project and for reasonable business records (like payment history). You can request deletion of your personal data at any time:

  • From the client portal, using "Request account deletion," or
  • By emailing {{ contact_email }}.

We'll act on verified requests within a reasonable time, except for records we're required to keep for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

Children's privacy

Resonate's services are for candidates, elected officials, and campaign or advocacy organizations — this site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

Any future marketing email from us will include an unsubscribe link. You can also always reach out directly to update or remove your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
