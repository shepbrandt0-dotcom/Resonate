Site tools such as the navigation chat are separate from client deliverables. Your speeches, notes, and strategy memos are human work.

Speech drafts and rewrites

Full speeches or targeted sections written or rewritten for a specific audience and moment. This includes campaign launches, town hall openings, convention style remarks, and shorter set pieces for video.

The process starts with your goal and constraints, then produces language that is clear under pressure. You receive a draft in the member portal, with room for one or more revision rounds depending on the package.

Delivery coaching

Notes and live or async coaching on how the speech is delivered: pacing, emphasis, pauses, and the energy in the room. The focus is removing the forced tone younger audiences notice immediately, while keeping substance intact.

Coaching pairs well with a draft so the words and the delivery support each other. Feedback is practical and specific, not generic presentation tips.

Messaging strategy

A focused memo on tone, framing, and message hierarchy for a race, issue, or event. It covers what must land, what to de-emphasize, and how the same core idea can travel from a formal speech to a short clip without losing integrity.

Useful when the problem is not a missing paragraph but a voice that does not fit the audience you need to reach.

Short form scripts

Sets of short scripts for social video, stories, or clipped remarks. Written for how people under thirty actually watch: fast hooks, plain language, and a single clear point.

Packages usually include several scripts so you can rotate topics without sounding like a template farm.

Town hall prep

Preparation for open forums: likely questions, response frames, and language that stays steady with mixed age audiences. Helps you answer hard questions without sliding into jargon or defensiveness.

Built for real rooms, not only televised debate formats.

Rapid response notes

Fast turnaround guidance when news moves and you need a public line that will not age poorly online. Short, precise, and written with younger voters and share culture in mind.

Best for clients who already have an active relationship or subscription so context is already shared.

Debate prep support

Message frames and practice language for debates and high pressure public exchanges. Draws on competitive debate experience to stress test clarity and composure.

Complements policy briefings from your campaign team rather than replacing them.

Monthly and yearly support

Ongoing capacity for active campaigns: priority turnaround, multiple deliverables, and a consistent voice across events. Monthly and yearly options are listed on the Pricing page.

Designed for teams that need a steady partner rather than one off projects.
