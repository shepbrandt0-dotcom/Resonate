Cookie Policy

The short version

This site uses exactly one cookie: an essential session cookie that keeps you signed into the client portal or admin area. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind.

The session cookie

When you log into the client portal or the admin area, this site sets a cookie that identifies your logged-in session. It's required for those areas to work — without it, the site can't tell you're logged in. It doesn't track you across other sites and isn't used for advertising. It's cleared when you log out or expires after a period of inactivity.

Third-party requests (not cookies we set)

Some pages load resources directly from other providers. Those providers may see standard technical information (like your IP address) when your browser requests their file — that's a normal part of how the web works, and separate from any cookie this site sets:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — page styling
  • Unsplash — one decorative background photo
  • A QR-code script, loaded only on the phone-demo widget in the admin dashboard and demo page

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and site data for this site at any time in your browser settings. Since the only cookie here is the essential login cookie, doing so will simply log you out.

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
